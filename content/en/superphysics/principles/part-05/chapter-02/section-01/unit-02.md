### Leakable Data

Leakable Data is information that is designed so that there is no detrminental consequence for the self or society if they were shared. 

Examples are:
- information about UFOs
- meditation techniques that are supposedly secret
- user data from the moneyless system of Superpeconomics


It is exactly the opposite of proprietary data, confidential data, private data, etc.

## The Psychic Horizon

Leakable Data is also a rehearsal for something stranger. If current trends hold—neural interfaces, ambient sensing, predictive models trained on everything—humanity is drifting toward a condition we might call *practical telepathy*: a world where minds are increasingly readable, and privacy becomes a physical impossibility rather than a policy choice.

In that world, the Leakable Data philosophy stops being a security tactic and becomes a way of life. If everyone can read everyone's mind, then the only sustainable strategy is to think thoughts you don't mind sharing—to make your inner life, like your database, leakable by design. The organizations that adopt Leakable Data today are, perhaps unknowingly, practicing for a future where the boundary between inside and outside dissolves entirely.

## The Takeaway

Hacking by AI is unstoppable. Minds, increasingly, will be readable. The winning move in both cases is the same: **don't build your survival around keeping secrets—build it around having nothing that needs to be kept.** Leakable Data is the first step toward that world, and it happens to make your security budget a lot smaller along the way. -->
