## Dialogue with the Neutrals

Last transmission, we gave you a preview of communicating with a member of another species. 

Though they are a member of the association, to remain neutral with regard to relations and communications regarding your world and several other worlds.

This is the opportunity for opening a dialogue with one of their representatives.

This will allow them to assess whether or not it would be of mutual benefit for them have dialogues of their own or add their energy to these dialogues.

Primarily they abstained because they did not really deem that any change would occur for creating such communication.

But they have, through invitation and partial observation, now been convinced to check in and to see if things have changed sufficiently to warrant their participation.

This does not mean that they consider themselves to be better than you. 

This is not a judgment call, but simply on their part a recognition of what they deem to be timely, fitting, appropriate for their civilization as well as your own with regard to such interactions and such happenstances.

Many of the civilizations that have been communicating with you in this methodology—and in particular many of those consciousnesses and entities that we have facilitated over time from time to time in these gatherings, in these transmissions—many of the entities that you have spoken to without our acting as a go-between have been relatively connected or relatively close, genetically speaking, to the idea of human history.

They are humanoid, human-like.

We all share with you similar genetic material. They have the head, 2 arms, 2 legs, 2 eyes. They are different in texture, though different in somewhat of their appearance, to be relatively understandable.

But this civilization is not human at all. 

They are 6 to 8 feet tall. They are not really human.

They are of a consciousness that is, in your terms, evolved going into a non-physical state, existing in a quasi-physical non-physical state.

Yet they do have a physiology and appearance. We will not describe them at this time because we do not want to put limitations in your mind or appearances. We wish the exchange of the information to be paramount—to be the focus—rather than their appearance.

In time, if they decide to begin to participate and interact, then a description or a definition of their appearance and their culture and their world may then become forthcoming. But until such time, let us focus on the idea of the dialogue and the interactions.

This interaction will take place in a very specific way through a "protocol."

There will be a great deal of protocol in this interaction, as befits their species, as befits their style of interaction. 

Also, we are allowed to tell you that what we are about to describe as the protocol for this interaction may be in and of itself—that is, the execution of how you handle this protocol—maybe in and of itself something that they will be watching for, something that they will be looking at, something that they will be assessing, and that may factor in to their decision as to whether or not to begin to participate.

The very act of interacting is something they are going to be looking at, as well as the information in the interaction.

So we will function as a go-between.

You will not even really be talking directly to one of them, but we will translate as a mediator, as a go-between, what they have to say, what you have to say. We will preface their information by the terms "they say" or "they ask," so that you will know when we are referring to their dialogue as opposed to our own.

### The Protocol

Choose a single representative who will then take the various statements and questions through a raising of hands.

But as each person is called upon who may have a question or a statement or a sharing for this being, then that person will state their question, but the representative alone will actually state the question or restate the question or repeat it for the purpose of the actual interaction.

The single representative that you choose will not have to be the only representative for the interaction. 

In other words, you can choose a representative and perhaps, for example, allow that representative to field three questions. 

You may then decide that you want to give someone else a chance to function as representative, but there will only be at any one time one representative who will address the questions to the being, to the neutral as we refer to them.

There can be a volunteer for the representative, but there must be—not majority decision backing that that person will be the representative—there must be unanimous decision. These beings, the neutrals, do not function by majority rule. Anything and everything that they do must be agreed upon unanimously by every single member. One abstaining member and there is no decision in that direction.

Therefore, it is their opinion, their feeling for their society, that to witness how you may handle this idea on your own terms will be something that they are looking at for their own purposes. Therefore, let us begin in some manner for you to decide to pick your first representative for this interaction. 

That can be again by volunteer and then by vote to back the volunteer, or you can decide some other way. But choose now.
