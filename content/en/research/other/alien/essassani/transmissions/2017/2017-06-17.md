### Trees and the Mycelial Network

Trees are like antennae protruding upwards into the sky from the Earth.

Deep underground, in the roots of trees and plants, there is another expression of this connective network — this social network. This is a type of growth of fungal filaments that attach to the roots and go from one tree to another, throughout the grove, throughout the forest. So in a forest where the trees are dense, all the trees are connected underground by their roots through this fungal mycelial network.

This is a true transport system underground for the trees of a forest. There is a vast amount of information and exchange going on under your feet when you walk through a forest — like an underground subway network that you may not even have been aware of, going on just a few feet beneath the surface.
