Electricity is the crude substance of Radiance that is made up of the quanta of electrons.

The natural phenomena of electricity is lightning. 

We classify all electric phenomena into 2:

1. Displacement as Static Electricity 

This is the displacement of electrons.

This includes Piezo Electricity.


2. Rotation as Current

This is the flow of electrons. This includes Electromagnetism.
