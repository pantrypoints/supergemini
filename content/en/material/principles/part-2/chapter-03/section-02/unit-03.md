Quantum tunneling happens when a particle passes through an energy barrier that, according to classical physics, it shouldn't have enough energy to cross.

This happens because mostly everything is empty space:
- the outside of the barrier is empty space of high vibration, called the aether
- the inside of the barrier is atomic space of low vibration, called the aetherspace


### How Quantum Tunneling Works: Density Sync

Particles (with slow spin) are sped up by increasing their density into a small space. 

This increases their spin to match that of the empty space on the other side. 

In stars, the small space of the star's core recreates the high energy density per space and therefore increases the spin to create a spin-match. 


Unlike quantum mechanics that uses wave-particle duality to explain quantum tunneling, Material Superphysics explains it as low density atomic space **matching or syncing** with the high density of the aether or empty space.

Tunneling probability drops exponentially with barrier width and height, because the chance for a sync becomes less.
