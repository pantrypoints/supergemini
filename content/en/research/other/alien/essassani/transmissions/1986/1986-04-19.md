### The Path of Transformation and Self-Acceptance

You are going through many of the Transformations that are taking place now on Earth.

You change the different methodologies, the different modes of operation that you find yourselves exploring. All these different modes of operation are all part and parcel of the idea of discovering yourself to be who you know yourselves to be. In this way, therefore, simply recognize that many times when you go through different modalities, when you change from one methodology to another in this way, simply because of the way you are used to reacting in your Society, many times you will negate or judge or cancel the idea or call it negative as to the idea you have previously been involved with. Now that you are involved in something else, many times you will tend to Discount the idea that you have already been previously involved in, thinking that it now in this way is, as you say, no good and that what you are doing now is better than what you were doing then.

But recognizing this way, many times the idea is that every single step you have ever taken, every single stepping stone, has gotten you to where you are. Thus, in this way, you will find that it is just as much of a break in your momentum, just as much of a stoppage of the flow of your continuity, to Discount and judge and negate anything you have ever done as it is to Discount anything that you could ever do. In this way, the entire idea, the entire path that you are, will in this way remain unbroken and constantly flowing to you and through you if you are willing to recognize that anything that could come to you is a part of the path you chose to be, and anything you have ever done is a part of the path you chose to be. For everything you have ever done has brought you to where you are now and therefore has served you in a positive way. There is no need to judge that certain things that you used to do are invalid compared to the things you are doing now simply because the things you used to do brought you to what you are now at this point.

Thus, the more you are willing to understand every single event in your life, every single experience, every single interaction, every single situation that you have interacted with as a part of the path you chose to be in your so-called past and in your so-called future, that is when you will allow yourself to fully experience your reality in the present moment. And in this way, you will allow every experience to be a consciously recognizable part of the path you chose to be, and you will feel yourself to be one unbroken whole, continuous idea in every given now moment that you create to exist.

Therefore, when you find that at some point in your life a certain source of information has meaning for you and then you simply find that the idea no longer has meaning, this does not mean that it is an opportunity necessarily to invalidate that source of information. For at the time it did serve you, and at the time that is the level you were on, and you did benefit from it, if only to recognize perhaps that the information that was coming to you may not represent the reality you know to be true for you. That does not mean it is not valid, for it was an opportunity to recognize what is true for you based upon allowing yourself the opportunity to see what is not true for you.

Now the distinction in terms of your outlook within these situations will determine how consciously aware you are of all the other experiences that come into your life. For to determine that something in your past has been invalid is to open up a higher degree of probability that something coming in from the future will also be looked at as not valid. Anytime you invalidate any portion of yourself, which includes all the experiences you have ever had, you invalidate the total idea of yourself and therefore do not let yourself be able to perceive that the ideas that are coming to you, the experiences and situations that are coming to you from the so-called future, are a part of your path. Many times you may become the idea of suspicious because of things that have happened in the past and not allow yourself to trust that they were a part of your path, and therefore not allow yourself to trust that some things coming into your life from the future will in this way also always be a part of your path. You may become suspicious that they could be interruptions in your path, which they cannot be unless you judge that they can.

In this way, therefore, simply recognize that if you find that something is no longer true for you, then simply take it as a recognition that you have changed or that the source of information has changed. But if it has changed, if you have changed, this is simply another opportunity to recognize what it is that is now true for you, what you have become. All you need to do once again is allow yourself to recognize that everything that has ever happened in your life or will ever happen in your life is a part of the path you chose to be. Having that Outlook and having that perspective and having that point of view and having that attitude is what will allow you to understand all the situations that come into your life because you will be willing to accept them as a part of the path, and thus you will replace your suspicion with fascination, with curiosity, with imagination, and with excitement. And those faculties are what will allow you clear, Clairvoyant, and conscious recognition of exactly what those situations do serve you in your life, how they fit into the path you chose to be, and allow your lives to become very transparent and a work of ease of creation rather than a struggle and a Strife to achieve. 

You always attach yourself firmly to the thing you judge. The idea is that you chose this reality in which to exist. 

In choosing this reality in which to exist, you did so for your own reasons, your own purposes, and you allowed yourself the opportunity to Simply recognize, therefore, that if you know that the life you have chosen to live is for your own reasons, then you can simply take it for granted and trust that every single thing you will encounter in this life must be a part of the path you chose to be.

For the universe does not do pointless and extraneous things. You can judge that it does, and it is only the Judgment that something is Superfluous that creates the effect of the seeming superfluousness.

But every situation is fundamentally neutral. No situation has built-in meaning; they are all blank, neutral circumstances. You supply the meaning to any situation.

In supplying your meaning, positive and/or negative, to Any Given blank neutral situation, you then, by the choice of that Outlook, by the choice of that perspective and attitude, determine what effect you will reap from that situation. Because positive outlook in, positive effect out. Negative outlook in, negative effect out.

But it is only your judgment of a situation as something that you do not think belongs in your life that allows you to have the experience as if that situation did not belong in your life. Only the Judgment causes the effect.

There are no interruptions in your life. You are complete and whole as a Creator, and everything, every idea you are, comes complete with all the ingredients and situations necessary for the manifestation and continuance of that idea in your reality.

Thus, when you are willing to understand that everything you experience, every situation you attract in your life, is a part of your path and therefore can be looked at with excitement—here is another part of me to discover, to add to the life that I am and live, the path I chose to be—then that is how that situation will affect your life, and you will be able to see how it fits in.
